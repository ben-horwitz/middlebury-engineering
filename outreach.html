Outreach

Community Outreach

We're dedicated to collaboration and innovation in our community. We've partnered with Women in Health Sciences and the Girl Scouts on logo-design and sticker-making workshops, and we're always open to helping with design needs, from props for a dance group to other creative requests.

Girl Scout outreach

We hosted a badge workshop for the local Middlebury Girl Scout troop. They learned about engineering, made their own toy cars, and earned a badge.

General workshops

Want a workshop for your club? We're happy to host a closed event for your org. Our keychain and sticker-making workshops have always been a big hit.

Miscellaneous requests

If you're part of the Middlebury community, college or town, we're happy to help with personal projects when we have the people. Need a prop for your dance troupe or an automated watering system for a community garden? Send us a message!

Interested in collaborating?

Whether you're a community group, a student organization, or a town member who needs a hand, reach out and tell us how we can work with you.
