Maker Space Workshops

Learn the tools, get signed off, and start making on your own.

Skills to bring your ideas to life

The Middlebury Engineering Society partners with the Maker Space to host hands-on workshops. So far we've trained students on the vinyl cutter, laser cutter, and 3D printers, helping them make everything from custom stickers and keychains to bottle openers.

You don't need an engineering background. Our workshops welcome students from all majors and experience levels. Once trained, you can use the Maker Space machines on your own, which opens the door to endless personal and academic projects.

Getting trained

Maker Space guidelines

We work closely with the Maker Space team so everyone can create safely and responsibly. Here's what you need to know:

  • Complete the Maker Space Pledge before your first workshop.
  • Respect the space: return tools and materials to their proper place.
  • Follow the official training created by the Maker Space director for each machine. Our workshops guide you through it.

Our workshops are built around these official training materials to make the process faster and easier. Once you've been trained and signed off, you're free to use the machines on your own.

We're also developing Learning Pathways: step-by-step progressions matched to your skill level and interests, taking you from beginner basics to advanced techniques.

Collaboration opportunities

We love teaming up with other campus groups on workshops that blend engineering with their interests. For example, we ran a logo design session with the Women in Health Science club and helped members turn their designs into custom stickers. If your club has an idea, we'd love to make it real.
