Maker Space Workshops

Learn the tools, get signed off, and start making on your own.

Skills to bring your ideas to life

The Middlebury Engineering Society partners with the Maker Space to host hands-on workshops. We've trained students on the vinyl cutter, laser cutter, and 3D printers, helping them make everything from custom stickers and keychains to bottle openers.

You don't need an engineering background. Our workshops welcome students from all majors and experience levels. Once trained, you can use the Maker Space machines on your own.

Getting trained

Maker Space guidelines

We work closely with the Maker Space team so everyone can create safely and responsibly:

  • Complete the Maker Space Pledge before your first workshop.
  • Respect the space: return tools and materials to their proper place.
  • Follow the official training created by the Maker Space director for each machine. Our workshops guide you through it.

Once you've been trained and signed off, you're free to use the machines on your own.

We run closed workshops for clubs and teams. Tell us what you have in mind.
