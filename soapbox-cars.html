Soapbox Cars

Get your hands dirty, learn the basics of cars, and race at the end of the year.

InterestsCars, working with friends, and getting comfortable with power tools.
SkillsTeamwork!
TimeLeaders: 2–3 hours a week. Participants: at least one drop-in hour a week.

What are soapbox cars?

A soapbox car is an engine-less vehicle and a great place to start learning the basics of how cars work. You'll get your hands dirty and show off your creativity while building these neat little cars.

How do I get involved?

No experience necessary! Come join us in the garage and we'll help you get started. We're currently stripping down and rebuilding old lawn mowers, which will become the frames of our vehicles for a race at the end of the school year.

We can't wait to meet you!
