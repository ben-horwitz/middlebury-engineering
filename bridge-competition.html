Design · Build · Crush

Bridge Building Competition

By the end of the semester, everyone will be able to design and fabricate a custom wooden truss bridge, then we load them until they break.

Learn engineering by building

The bridge competition is our main program each fall. Over about eight weekly sessions in the Maker Space, teams of three or four learn the theory and the tools behind real structural design, starting with no experience at all.

Along the way you'll pick up skills every engineer uses: CAD, laser cutting, woodworking, and the basics of stress and strain. By the time you're done, you're most of the way to being certified on the Maker Space laser cutter.

WhenWednesdays, 4:30–6:00 PM, fall semester
WhereMaker Space, Johnson Memorial Center
Teams3–4 people
ExperienceNone needed
What you'll learn

Three skills, one bridge

CAD in Onshape

Design your bridge in Onshape, a free, browser-based CAD tool. Learn sketches, constraints, parts and assemblies, and turn your design into files ready to cut.

Laser cutting

Learn how the laser cutter works, how to run it safely, and how to prepare files in Adobe Illustrator. Your first cut is a keychain; your last is a bridge.

Stress and strain

An intro to solid mechanics: stress and strain, tension and compression, tolerances, and why trusses like the Warren, Pratt and Howe carry load so well.

The semester

Week by week

Intro to engineering

What engineers actually do, the different fields, and a preview of the bridge you'll build by the end of the semester.

Engineering basics and bridge research

Measuring and tolerancing, stress (σ) and strain (ε), and tension versus compression. Teams research classic trusses (Warren, Pratt, Howe and A-truss), work out which members are in tension and compression, and present to the club.

Laser cutting

How laser cutters work, shop safety, and an Adobe Illustrator walkthrough. Everyone designs and cuts their own keychain, which covers most of the Maker Space laser-cutter training.

Intro to CAD

Zero experience required. Sketching, fully defining sketches with dimensions and constraints, extrusions, and parts in Onshape.

Intro to the woodshop

Grain direction, joints, friction fits, gluing, sanding and clamping. Teams run a joint destruction test to find out which glue and joint actually holds.

Designing your bridge in CAD

Guided modeling of the standard deck and crossmembers, then each team designs its own side-truss profile.

CAD assembly and analysis

Put the whole bridge together in an assembly, check that everything fits, and look at how the load flows through it.

Build time

Laser cut, glue and assemble, with TAs on hand to troubleshoot.

Crush day

Every bridge is loaded until it fails. Then there's a results night with pizza. Bring your friends.
