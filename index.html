Mission Statement

Engineering for everyone

The Middlebury Engineering Society is dedicated to making engineering education and hands-on experience accessible to all students. Through technical workshops and community outreach, we help peers build skills, solve real problems, and find their way into engineering (even at a school without an engineering major).

Whether you're designing your first part in CAD or planning your path to the Dartmouth dual-degree, there's a place for you here.

What we do

Get involved

Bridge Competition

An eight-week series in Onshape CAD, laser cutting, and truss analysis that ends with custom plywood truss bridges, culminating with a crush test.

Workshops

Makerspace trainings that get students certified on 3D printers, laser cutters, and vinyl cutters.

Outreach

Partnering with campus clubs and local groups like the Middlebury Girl Scouts and Future Researchers Club.

Dual Degree

A yearly panel and peer advice for students pursuing engineering through the dual degree programs.
