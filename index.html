Mission Statement

Engineering for everyone

The Middlebury Engineering Society is dedicated to making engineering education and hands-on experience accessible to all students. Through technical workshops, collaborative projects, and community outreach initiatives, we empower students to build skills, solve real-world problems, and expand engineering opportunities across campus and beyond.

Whether you're learning to 3D print your first keychain or leading a multi-week build, there's a place for you here.

What we do

Three pillars

Education

Biweekly Maker Space trainings on 3D printers, laser cutters, vinyl cutters and Adobe Illustrator.

Projects

Collaborative, student-led engineering builds, like our soapbox cars made from old lawn mowers.

Community Outreach

Bringing STEM to the wider community with partners like the Girl Scouts and Women in Health Sciences.
Important update

From Formula Hybrid to MES

Starting in Spring 2024, the Middlebury Formula Hybrid Club shifted gears and became the Middlebury Engineering Society.

The Formula Hybrid car is no longer our focus and won't be competing in Formula College Racing this year. If you're interested in working on the car, email us at engineering@middlebury.edu.
