Mission Statement

Engineering for everyone

The Middlebury Engineering Society is dedicated to making engineering education and hands-on experience accessible to all students. Through technical workshops, competitions, and community outreach, we help students build skills, solve real problems, and find their way into engineering, even at a school without an engineering major.

Whether you're designing your first part in CAD or planning your path to Dartmouth, there's a place for you here.

Dual Degree

Our yearly panel and advice for students pursuing the Middlebury–Dartmouth dual degree in engineering.

Bridge Competition

Eight weeks of CAD, laser cutting, and stress and strain, ending with a plywood truss bridge crush test.

Workshops

Maker Space trainings on 3D printers, laser cutters, and vinyl cutters, open to every major.

Outreach

Bringing STEM to the wider community with partners like the Girl Scouts and Women in Health Sciences.
